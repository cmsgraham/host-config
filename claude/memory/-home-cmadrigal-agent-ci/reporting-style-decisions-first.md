Told directly on 2026-09-09, after a day where several asks (Phase 8 ordering,
Node 22, queueing tasks) sat unanswered for hours because they were buried in
paragraph six of a long message: *"sometimes the text you post is a lot and
dense and some decisions get lost in it… we don't delay the process."*

**Format to use:**

1. **NEEDS YOU** first — what only they can decide or run. Max 3 items. Say
   "nothing" when there is nothing, so its absence is informative.
2. **Status** — two or three lines. What is running, what landed, what failed.
3. **Detail** — only when asked, or when it changes what they would do.

**Use the question prompt for real decisions** rather than prose. It renders as
pickable options and cannot be skimmed past. Prose questions were missed
repeatedly; a question prompt was answered immediately.

**Why:** the cost is not the reading, it is the delay. A decision they never
saw is a queue that idles — and idle time is already the single largest loss in
this pipeline's own stats (38% of calendar).

**How to apply:** long-form is still right for a report they asked for (an
overnight summary, an evaluation, a publish guide — put those in a file). It is
wrong for a routine status update. When in doubt: what must they do, what is
running, stop. See [[user-goal-autonomous-devinfra]].
