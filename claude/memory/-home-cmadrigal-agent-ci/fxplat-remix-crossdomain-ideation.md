Cristian 2026-10-21 (after carry/momentum failed): "we need to figure out and mix ideas that already exist,
improving them or looking at things in a newer way - that's remixing. We explored average or a bit above average
ideas; now we have immense power: find the connections nobody else has seen, things nobody thought have to do with
each other; find influencers, researchers, Nobel prizes, even biologists, doctors, sociologists; inspiration from
Einstein and Satoshi on how they connected the dots."
**Why:** the well-known FX edges (breakout, carry, momentum) are in the book of shame; he wants the Caleidoscopio
to be genuinely creative, cross-disciplinary, not a rehash of finance textbooks.
**How to apply:** the ideation layer (TASK-11.17 seeds, 11.16 library) must include a CROSS-DOMAIN mechanism library
(ecology, epidemiology, physics of phase transitions/turbulence, network/information cascades, behavioural economics,
neuroscience, queueing/auction theory, consensus/incentive design) with each mechanism mapped to a testable FX
hypothesis in the catalogue grammar; plus REMIX operators (combine two existing conditions / transplant a mechanism
to a new frame or market). High temperature = far analogies. Honesty stays: every idea still faces costs, the luck
filter and the 20-try budget. Related: [[fxplat-hallazgos-caleidoscopio]], [[fxplat-discarded-strategies-book]].
