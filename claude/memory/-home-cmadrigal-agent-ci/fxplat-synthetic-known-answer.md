2026-10-28 ROOT CAUSE FOUND (data-backed, the real one). Instrumented E-S trades under null/up/down drift: the planted drift shifts ENTRY and EXIT by the same amount, so exit-entry (=> r_gross, exit_kind) is UNCHANGED. The edge has no effect on fast-exit trades. Mechanism: a planted edge is a STEADY per-minute level drift carried forward forever (plant.py docstring §6.1 "carried to every later minute"). It biases a trade only if the trade HOLDS long enough for the drift to accumulate a differential between entry and exit. E-A (brk_2R) and E-B (fixed_h, 24h) hold long -> works. E-S (rev_1R) exits FAST at +/-1R -> the drift over the short hold is negligible -> no bias, regardless of mu sign (up HURTS via later-trade distortion, down is ~flat at the -0.25 cost baseline). To bias a fast exit you need a large drift RATE, but a large rate carried forward across 2579 events overflows. THE KNOT: fast-exit edges need a strong LOCAL bias; the persistent carry turns strong-local into global-overflow. FIX = mean-reverting (decaying) carry: the drift reverts toward the null path over a half-life, so a strong per-trade bias does NOT accumulate (no overflow), causality preserved (no pre-event move), E-A/E-B still biased over their long holds. Open design param: the decay half-life (tension between long-hold D1 edges wanting a long half-life and frequent daily events wanting a short one; per-symbol carry aggregates all edges so can't be per-edge easily). Implementing a global ~3-day half-life as a first cut; verify empirically then tune.
