Cristian 2026-10-20: "I think 1000$ will be my start, using the best proven [broker], once we have a positive
perspective of edge on the forecast - not saying we have to win but at least major chances to win; start trading
to just cover costs doesn't make sense."
- Live start = US$1,000 real account; research ruler stays US$5,000 (1R=US$50) but every report/analyzer output
  should also show results at US$1,000 (1R=US$10 at 1%, US$5 at the 0.5% live-minimum of prompt §12 Phase 8).
- Broker must charge proportionally (no IB-style US$2 minimum: at US$1k it is 0.4R/trade): Dukascopy (Swiss bank,
  equity tier <5k = US$35/M/side) or IC Markets Raw (US$3/lot/side, offshore entity). Micro-lot granularity (1,000
  units) makes H1 sizing fine at US$1k; D1 (200-pip stops) cannot be sized correctly at US$1k / 1%.
- Go-live gate in his words: a positive expected result AFTER costs with a high probability (not break-even).
  Maps onto G3 + paper evidence; the Caleidoscopio P1/P2 probabilities are the natural "chances" readout.
